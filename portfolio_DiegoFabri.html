About

I'm Diego, a designer from Antwerp. I studied cross-media design before moving into product development, and I like to work where those two worlds meet: objects, tools and the digital experiences around them.

I'm fascinated by biomimicry, organic form and well-made things. Next to my studies I run a small independent studio for graphic design, campaigns and AR/XR experiences.

I'm currently finishing my Master in Product Development at the University of Antwerp and I'm open to collaborations that connect design and technology.
